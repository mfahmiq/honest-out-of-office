- C10 vs C7 → **C10**. C7's "whoever shouts loudest" is funny but borderline rude; C10's self-deprecating "exactly as ignored" stays polite.
- C9 vs C8 → **C9**. C8's "isn't three weeks late" is honest; C9's "I need this break more than my inbox needs me" is warmer and more quotable.
